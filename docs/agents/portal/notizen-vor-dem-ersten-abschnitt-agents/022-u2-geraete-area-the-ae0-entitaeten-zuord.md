Ausgelagert aus `frontend/portal/AGENTS.md` am 05.09.2026 (Abschnitt Nr. 1, Punkt 022).

- **U2 "Geräte" area = the AE0 "Entitäten & Zuordnung" screen, built (design `data/vp-ems-ui-overhaul/report.md` §3, epic UO #513, builds on U1).** `pages/EntitaetenSection.tsx` (the U1 `entitaeten` tab, label "Geräte", route UNCHANGED `#/anlage/{id}/entitaeten`) became a three-section area: **1. Ihre Geräte** (the reused entity cards + NEW role pills from `api.topology` and NEW strategy chips from `api.entityStrategies` linking into Steuerung), **2. Rollen & Zuordnung** (the NEW keystone screen over the AE1 backend — role boxes PV-Erzeugung/Speicher/Netz/Verbraucher with member chips, Σ, **maßgeblich ✓** on the primary, ＋ zuordnen, "Automatisch zugeordnet" default state), **3. Vom Gerät gemeldet** (the adoption bridge — edge-reported `localSetup` sources not yet an entity, adoptable in one click, **admin-only first increment**; customers see the honest "VoltPilot richtet ein" hint), plus a bottom "Physische Verbindung" pointer to the device `:8484`.
  - **ALL derivation is the pure `src/rollen.ts` (`rollen.test.ts`):** `roleBoxes`/`rolePillsFor`/`assignableCapabilities`/`isAutoAssigned` (compares each capability's resolved role to `defaultRole` from `topology.ts` — no backend "is-default" flag exists), the assignment PUT builders `assignToRole`/`setPrimaryAssignment`/`resetAssignments` (blank role clears the override), and the adoption helpers `adoptableSources`/`adoptedSources`/`inverterSetup`/`suggestEntityType`(role+brand→catalog type: go-e→wallbox, pv-generation→producer, grid-meter→grid-meter)/`sourceRoleLabel`/`sourceSummary`. Role hues reuse the `--vp-flow-*` tokens (PV orange, Speicher green, Netz teal, Verbraucher purple). CSS block "U2 «Geräte» area" in `index.css` (`.vp-role-pill`/`.vp-strategy-chip`/`.vp-role-box`/`.vp-role-member`/`.vp-massgeblich`/`.vp-gemeldet-item`) — every card/box/member `min-width:0`, grids collapse to 1fr @720px; verified zero horizontal overflow at 360px.
  - **Customer writes go through the RLS-fenced `api.setTopologyRoles(siteId, assignments)` → `PUT /api/v1/sites/{id}/topology-roles`** (a role is presentation-level and never widens control, so the customer twin needs no extra gate — mirrors `SiteFlowController`). The PUT returns the recomputed `SiteTopology`, lifted back into the section so it stays in sync. Adoption is `entitiesApi.adopt(siteId, {sourceId, entityType, label?, maxPowerKw?, capacityKwp?, registryUnitId?})` → admin-only `POST /admin/sites/{id}/v2-entities/adopt` (idempotent per sourceId; a producer's kWp sums into the aggregate `asset.pv`, the retired panel's job). `SiteEntity` gained `edgeSourceId`; `EntityLocalSetup` gained `role`/`brand`/`adoptedEntityId` (the adoption matcher).
  - **`ErzeugerSourcesPanel` is RETIRED (§3.4):** removed from `AnlageTechnik` "Mein Gerät" (now a one-line pointer to Geräte); its list-of-sources job → "Ihre Geräte", its master-data capture (kWp/MaStR) → the adoption drawer. Technik keeps everything else. `api.measurementPoints`/`addMeasurementPoint`/`deleteMeasurementPoint` stay in `api.ts` (endpoint still live) but no component uses them. Tests: `rollen.test.ts` + `pages/EntitaetenSection.test.tsx` (renders the three sections, admin adopt CTA vs customer hint).
